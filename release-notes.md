# 1.0.0-beta.3

- Adds `Patcher.PatchesFromMethod(MethodInfo)` to retrieve patch configurations from an attribute-defined patch without registering it.
- Updates the bundled Harmony for RimWorld 1.7 to 3.0.0-rc.3.
- Builds the RimWorld 1.7 integration against the 1.7 reference assemblies.

Harmony 3 support remains incomplete and subject to change.
